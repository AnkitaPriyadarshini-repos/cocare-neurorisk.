CO CARE : Cognitive Risk Screening ML Showcase

OASIS Demo / LASI-DAD Ready

Publication-grade validation, leakage-free cross-validation, and responsible AI transparency.

⚠️ DEMO EVALUATION ONLY: Models were trained on the OASIS longitudinal cohort (Washington University, USA). They have NOT been validated on LASI-DAD or Odia/Indian rural populations and must not be used for screening real patients. This is strictly a demonstration of an explainable, privacy-preserving screening architecture.
